About Me

I'm Elias

Mechanical Engineer and Ph.D. Researcher

I am a Mechanical Engineer and Ph.D. student at Louisiana Tech University in Ruston, Louisiana, specializing in Micro and Nanoscale Engineering. My doctoral research focuses on nanoparticle-enhanced concrete to improve mechanical strength, durability, and long-term service life. My experience includes mechanical research and development, robotic mechanism design, finite element analysis, prototyping, fabrication, and dynamic testing. I also work with Louisiana Tech University's Industrial Assessment Center, participating in industrial energy assessments and developing recommendations to improve equipment performance, energy efficiency, and material use.
