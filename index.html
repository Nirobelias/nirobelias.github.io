About Me

I'm Elias

Mechanical Engineer | Ph.D. Researcher

I am a Mechanical Engineer and Ph.D. student at Louisiana Tech University in Ruston, Louisiana, specializing in Micro and Nanoscale Engineering. My doctoral research focuses on nanoparticle-enhanced concrete to improve mechanical strength, durability, and long-term service life.

I am interested in applying engineering fundamentals, experimental research, and data-driven methods to practical engineering problems.

My professional experience spans mechanical research and development, robotic mechanism design, finite element analysis, prototyping, fabrication, and dynamic testing. Through Louisiana Tech University's Industrial Assessment Center, I also contribute to industrial energy assessments and develop practical recommendations for improving equipment performance, energy efficiency, and material use.

I am registered as an Engineer-in-Training (EIT) in Maryland and have passed the NCEES Principles and Practice of Engineering examination in HVAC and Refrigeration.
